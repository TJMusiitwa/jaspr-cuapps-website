# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Decision-makers at UK credit unions and mutuals (CEOs, boards, operations leads) evaluating whether to commission a bespoke member mobile app or the CU Chat AI assistant. They are risk-aware, budget-conscious, and often not deeply technical; they need confidence that CU Apps is credible, premium, and understands the credit-union sector. Primary conversion: booking a consultation (Calendly) or requesting a free demo.

## Product Purpose

CU Apps' marketing site. It showcases bespoke mobile app development and the CU Chat AI product, explains the delivery process, presents testimonials and client logos, and converts visitors into booked consultations. Success = a visitor trusts the brand enough to book a call.

## Brand Personality

Trusted, premium, approachable — with a bold, modern, tech-forward edge. Financial-sector credibility with warmth; confident about AI without hype. Voice is plain-spoken and benefit-led, not jargon-heavy.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS-template look (identical card grids, hero-metric blocks, gradient text).
- Stuffy legacy-bank aesthetics (navy-and-gold formality, dense corporate copy).
- AI-hype styling (glassmorphism everywhere, neon gradients).

## Design Principles

1. **Earn trust visually** — polish, contrast, and real product imagery over decoration; the site itself is proof of build quality.
2. **Two brands, one system** — blue carries CU Apps surfaces, purple carries CU Chat surfaces; never mix them arbitrarily.
3. **Show the product** — device frames, real screenshots, and concrete process steps beat abstract illustration.
4. **One job per page** — every page drives toward booking a consultation or demo.
5. **Fast and calm** — static-rendered, light motion, no scroll-jacking; performance is part of the premium feel.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA target: ≥4.5:1 body-text contrast, keyboard-navigable interactive elements, reduced-motion alternatives for all animation (already partially in place via `prefers-reduced-motion` gating).
