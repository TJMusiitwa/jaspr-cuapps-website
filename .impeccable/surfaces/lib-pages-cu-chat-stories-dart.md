# CU Chat stories (/cu-chat/stories)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: credit-union CEOs and ops leads weighing CU Chat. Job: see peers who already run a named CU Chat bot, then book a call. Proof: ten verbatim testimonials from cu.chat/portfolio, three 4-page case-study PDFs (hosted in web/case-studies), bot character art and in-context shots, client logos. Constraints: purple CU Chat shell; quotes stay verbatim; the only claims are the clients' own.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every chatbot is a named colleague: the page is a staff roll of Harry, Penny, Honey, Faira and the rest, beside the credit union that took them on. It refuses the anonymous testimonial grid and carousel.

OWN-WORLD: The CU Chat purple shell: purple-deep fields, lilac paper, white, Outfit. Bot names are set at display scale like signatures, with character art at portrait scale. Structure comes from ruled hairline rows, not cards.

STORY: A CEO sees peers name their bot, reads three worked cases with their PDFs, scans seven more voices, and books a call.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left seven columns: the headline naming ten colleagues, a lede and Book a call. Right five columns: a roster listing every bot name with its credit union, each linking to its story. The top of the Heritage/Harry feature shows at the fold.

FORM: Three features, then the roll. Position 3 of 7 on the ranked list. Seed key 81ea0fb5.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
